Policy Audit & Coverage Review Services

Uncover hidden exclusions, eliminate bloated carrier fees, and benchmark your existing policies against today's top market rates.

When Was the Last Time an Expert Stress-Tested Your Insurance Policies?

Over 73% of life and health insurance policies in America are obsolete. Life changes—such as home purchases, births, divorces, salary increases, or career promotions—often leave existing coverage dangerously misaligned. Furthermore, insurance underwriting algorithms have dramatically modernized, meaning policies purchased 5 to 10 years ago may be costing you thousands of dollars in unnecessary annual premiums.

What Our 25-Point Policy Audit Examines

Hidden Exclusion & Rider Discovery

We scrutinize the fine print for restrictive war, aviation, mental health, or high-deductible clauses that could trigger claim denials.

2026 Competitive Market Benchmarking

We match your exact coverage specifications against 40+ leading carriers to identify instant premium reduction opportunities.

Beneficiary & Estate Review

Verify primary and contingent designations to prevent costly probate delays, outdated ex-spouse designations, or tax complications.

Carrier Solvency & Claims Rating

Evaluate your current insurer’s A.M. Best, Moody’s, and Fitch financial strength ratings to ensure guaranteed claims solvency.

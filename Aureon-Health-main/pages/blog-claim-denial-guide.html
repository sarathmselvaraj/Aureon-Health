What to Do When an Insurance Carrier Denies a Medical Claim

By Sarah Kim (Claims Advocacy Director) • Sept 5, 2026 • 4 Min Read
Critical Fact:

Industry audits indicate that over 52% of initial health insurance claim denials are due to clerical or medical billing code mismatches (ICD-10 or CPT typos) and can be overturned on first appeal.

The 4-Stage Appeal Blueprint

Receiving a denial letter or an unexpected hospital bill is intimidating, but you have legally protected rights under federal and state insurance laws. Follow this proven 4-stage process:

STAGE 1
Decode the EOB Denial Reason

Carefully inspect the Denial Code on your Explanation of Benefits (EOB). Common codes include "Prior Authorization Missing", "Experimental Procedure", or "Not Medically Necessary".

STAGE 2
Request a Peer-to-Peer Review

Ask your attending physician's billing coordinator to schedule a "Peer-to-Peer" teleconference with the carrier's medical director to provide clinical rationale directly.

STAGE 3
Submit Internal Appeal Packet

Include the medical chart notes, a signed Letter of Medical Necessity from your specialist, FDA treatment documentation, and a formal narrative rebuttal.

STAGE 4
Independent External Review

If internal appeals fail, state laws guarantee your right to an Independent External Review (IRO) where unbiased outside doctors review your case. Their decision is binding on the carrier.

"Never accept a medical denial as the final word. Our claims advocacy team overturns seven out of ten denied claims simply by enforcing the carrier's own published clinical guidelines."

Key Deadlines You Cannot Miss

  • Filing Window: Most carriers require formal internal appeals to be submitted within 180 days of receiving the initial denial notice.
  • Carrier Response Time: Insurers must respond within 30 days for treatments already received, or within 72 hours for urgent medical care.
  • Certified Mail: Always send appeal documentation via certified mail with signature confirmation or through the insurer's authenticated online portal.

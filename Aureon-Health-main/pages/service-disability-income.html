Disability Income Protection Solutions

Replace up to 70% of your gross earnings tax-free if illness, injury, or surgery prevents you from practicing your profession.

Your Ability to Earn an Income is Your Greatest Financial Asset

Over the course of a 30-year career, a professional earning $100,000 annually will generate more than $3,000,000 in gross lifetime wealth. Yet while almost everyone insures their $400,000 house and $40,000 car, only 20% insure their multi-million dollar paycheck engine. Disability insurance provides guaranteed monthly cash income if an unforeseen injury, back disorder, cardiac condition, or illness sidelines you.

Core Policy Highlights & Protections

True "Own-Occupation" Definition

You receive full disability benefits if you cannot perform the specialized duties of your specific career, even if you teach or work elsewhere.

100% Tax-Free Monthly Checks

When you pay premiums with personal after-tax dollars, every monthly disability benefit check is delivered completely free of federal and state income taxes.

Future Purchase Option (FPO)

Increase your monthly disability benefit limit as your income advances over your career without ever repeating a medical exam.

Flexible Elimination Waiting Periods

Customize your waiting period (30, 60, 90, or 180 days) to coordinate seamlessly with your existing emergency savings account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Workers' Compensation only covers injuries sustained directly on the job site—yet over 90% of long-term disabilities are caused by everyday illnesses (cancer, heart disease, spinal issues) unrelated to work. Furthermore, SSDI rejects over 65% of initial claims and requires total inability to perform ANY job in the national economy.

"Any-Occupation" policies only pay if you cannot perform any gainful work whatsoever (even answering phones). "True Own-Occupation" policies pay 100% of your monthly benefit if you cannot perform the duties of your chosen specialty (such as surgeon, architect, or software engineer), even if you teach or choose another non-manual role.

You can tailor your benefit duration based on your retirement timeline. Popular options include 2 years, 5 years, 10 years, or all the way until full retirement age (Age 65 or Age 67).

Why Independent Brokerage Beats Captive Insurance Agents Every Single Time

Most people do not realize that when they call a household insurance agency (such as State Farm, Farmers, or Allstate), those agents are contractually "captive"—legally obligated to push only their employer’s specific policies, regardless of price or coverage limits.

Aureon Health was established with a singular fiduciary premise: we represent you, the client, not the insurance carrier. As an independent brokerage, our automated underwriting engines scan policies across 40+ A-rated insurance providers (Mutual of Omaha, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, Lincoln Financial, Guardian, Humana) to identify the absolute best coverage at the lowest possible monthly rate.

Our Fiduciary Commitment to You:
  • Zero Sales Quotas: Our advisors receive fixed salaries, not product-skewed incentives.
  • Standardized State-Filed Rates: You pay the exact same regulated rate (or less) than going direct.
  • Lifetime Concierge Advocacy: When claim time comes, our legal team fights the carrier on your behalf.

Your deductible is the annual dollar amount you pay for covered medical care before your health plan begins sharing costs. A copay is a fixed fee for routine doctor visits or prescriptions, while coinsurance is a percentage split (e.g. 80/20). Your out-of-pocket maximum is the annual ceiling; once met, the insurer pays 100% of all covered medical expenses.
